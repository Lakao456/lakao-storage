Privacy policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

Lakao Storage is a personal tool used only by its owner to back up and organise the owner's own files in the owner's own Google Drive.

  • What it accesses: the owner's Google Drive files and folders, through Google's Drive API, so it can upload, list, verify and move them.
  • Where data goes: files travel only between the owner's computer and the owner's Google Drive. No data is sent to the developer or to any third party.
  • What it stores: the sign-in token is stored only on the owner's computer. No analytics, tracking or advertising.
  • Sharing: nothing is sold, shared or disclosed.
  • Removing access: access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.
